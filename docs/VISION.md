# Reverence: vision and art direction

Reverence is sublime and epic, with tender cosmic love and transcendence: a letter to the sacred, to the
source, and to all life. Every track works toward that feeling.

## The target

repository). It is sorted into the categories below; critics and blind judges compare our captures with
those frames, category by category. Your prompt lists the reference files and the checklist for your

**The cosmic web must not regress.** The creator loves the opening cosmic web as it is ("it looks so beautiful").
Only the cosmic track changes `src/modes/cosmic/**`, and every one of its fixes is checked by a blind before/after
judge on fixed views and reverted unless the new look wins. Global changes (post-processing, UI, engine, shared
shaders) must leave the cosmic view unchanged.

## Art direction rules (apply everywhere)

1. The sky takes two thirds of the frame. The figure is small, near the bottom, there for scale.
2. Two hues per scene, plus one warm accent: the explorer's red scarf, a lantern, a lit window.
3. Space is true black. Night is deep indigo and still readable: starlight, glowing plants, lit ground.
4. Depth comes from haze. Each layer back is paler and cooler than the one in front.
5. Light does the acting: one shaft into a dark hall, one low sun, one lit doorway.
6. Stars and nebulae tint the space around them. A black disc keeps a thin, bright, coloured edge.
7. A neighbouring world can fill half the sky. Rings are fine parallel arcs from horizon to horizon, visible by day.
8. Giants are mostly haze and silhouette; their feet are clearer than their heads.
9. Weather is an event or a reward: a wall of dust, rain as a veil, a rainbow after the storm.
10. Each world has one luminous landmark you can see from anywhere.
11. Wonders are framed by an arch, a doorway or a cave mouth, and still water doubles the sky.

## What the creator's frames keep returning to

* Two people sitting close on a gentle, long-legged giant, watching something vast (the north star).
* A ring of light as the sign of the source: eclipse, black hole, portal, halo, mandala, the same shape at every scale.
* Small companions (a glowing creature, a cat, a fox) and gentle giants that lower their heads to you.
* Lighthouses, hearths and warm windows as home; resting and looking out over a view.
* A vast tree made of light; one great mountain; places where land and stone float free.
* Sacred architecture reached by a processional way; ruins softened by moss, flowers and water.
* Festivals, music and small lives in towns at dusk.
